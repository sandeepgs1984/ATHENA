# Architecture Review

## Summary

ID-0001 separates identity from organization by introducing stable identifiers independent of namespace or storage.

---

## Strengths

- Stable identity
- Human-readable
- Machine-readable
- Supports long-term evolution

---

## Risks

Identifier formats should remain stable to avoid widespread migration.

---

## Recommendation

Never recycle published identifiers, even if the corresponding knowledge asset is retired.
