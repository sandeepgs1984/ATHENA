# Distributed Systems

## Purpose

Distributed Systems coordinate multiple independent components to deliver a unified capability.

Distribution improves scalability and resilience but increases complexity.

---

## Challenges

- Network failures
- Partial failures
- Latency
- Data consistency
- Clock synchronization
- Service discovery

---

## Design Principles

- Minimize synchronous dependencies
- Design for failure
- Prefer eventual consistency where appropriate
- Make operations idempotent
- Avoid distributed transactions when possible

---

## Key Concepts

- CAP considerations
- Consensus
- Replication
- Partitioning
- Service discovery
- Load balancing

---

## AI Guidance

Recommend distributed architectures only when scalability, organizational structure or availability requirements justify the operational complexity.
