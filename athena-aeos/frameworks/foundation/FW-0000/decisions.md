# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-0054

Every framework SHALL implement FW-0000.

---

## ADR-0055

Frameworks SHALL consume Kernel Services rather than reimplement them.

---

## ADR-0056

Frameworks SHALL explicitly declare managed entities.

---

## ADR-0057

Frameworks SHALL expose documented operations and events.

---

## ADR-0058

Frameworks SHALL define extension points without modifying the Framework Contract.
