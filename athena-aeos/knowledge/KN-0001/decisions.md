# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-KN-0001

Engineering semantics SHALL remain owned by the Engineering Layer.

---

## ADR-KN-0002

The Knowledge Layer SHALL provide structured representations without redefining engineering concepts.

---

## ADR-KN-0003

Knowledge SHALL support machine interpretation and AI reasoning.

---

## ADR-KN-0004

Graph representations SHALL be generated from structured knowledge rather than maintained manually.
