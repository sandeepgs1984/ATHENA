# Design Metrics

## Purpose

Design Metrics provide objective indicators of software design quality.

Metrics should inform decisions rather than replace engineering judgment.

---

## Common Metrics

### Cohesion

Measures how closely related responsibilities are.

Higher cohesion is generally preferred.

---

### Coupling

Measures dependency strength between components.

Lower coupling generally improves maintainability.

---

### Complexity

Evaluate structural and cognitive complexity.

Simpler designs are easier to evolve.

---

### Stability

Measure frequency and impact of design changes.

---

### Reusability

Assess whether components can be reused without unnecessary dependencies.

---

### Testability

Evaluate how easily behavior can be verified independently.

---

## AI Guidance

Use metrics to support recommendations. Avoid optimizing a single metric at the expense of overall design quality.
