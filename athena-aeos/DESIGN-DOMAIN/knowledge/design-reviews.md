# Design Reviews

## Purpose

Design Reviews evaluate whether a proposed design satisfies business goals while remaining maintainable and adaptable.

---

## Review Areas

- Responsibility assignment
- Cohesion
- Coupling
- Dependencies
- Module boundaries
- Interfaces
- Extensibility
- Testability
- Simplicity

---

## Key Questions

- Is the design understandable?
- Can it evolve safely?
- Is abstraction justified?
- Are responsibilities explicit?
- Are dependencies appropriate?

---

## Outputs

- Review findings
- Risks
- Recommendations
- Improvement actions

---

## AI Guidance

Explain every recommendation using evidence from the design. Avoid subjective preferences without clear engineering justification.
