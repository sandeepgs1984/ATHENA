# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-ART-0001

Artifacts SHALL represent persistent engineering outputs.

---

## ADR-ART-0002

Artifact management SHALL remain independent of storage implementations.

---

## ADR-ART-0003

Artifacts SHALL support provenance and traceability.

---

## ADR-ART-0004

Published artifacts SHOULD be treated as immutable, with changes represented through new versions.
