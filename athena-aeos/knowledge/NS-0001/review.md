# Architecture Review

## Summary

NS-0001 establishes a consistent organizational structure for AEOS.

Namespaces improve discoverability and repository scalability while remaining independent of semantic meaning and identity.

---

## Strengths

- Simple hierarchy
- Technology independent
- Scalable
- Easy to understand

---

## Risks

Namespaces should remain coarse-grained. Deep hierarchies can reduce usability.

---

## Recommendation

Prefer stable, domain-oriented namespaces over implementation-specific structures.
