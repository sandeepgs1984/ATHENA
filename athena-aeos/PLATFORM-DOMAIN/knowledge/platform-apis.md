# Platform APIs

## Purpose

Platform APIs expose reusable engineering capabilities through stable, well-defined interfaces.

---

## Principles

- Consistent contracts
- Versioning
- Authentication
- Authorization
- Observability
- Backward compatibility

---

## Typical Capabilities

- Configuration
- Secrets
- Identity
- Feature flags
- Service registry
- Audit events

---

## Design Considerations

- Simplicity
- Discoverability
- Reliability
- Scalability

---

## AI Guidance

Recommend Platform APIs for shared engineering capabilities rather than embedding platform logic inside applications.
