# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-GOV-0001

Governance SHALL define organizational authority rather than implementation behavior.

---

## ADR-GOV-0002

Governance SHALL coordinate reviews without owning engineering entities.

---

## ADR-GOV-0003

Governance SHALL operate through transparent and traceable decision-making.

---

## ADR-GOV-0004

Governance outcomes SHALL be auditable and versioned.
