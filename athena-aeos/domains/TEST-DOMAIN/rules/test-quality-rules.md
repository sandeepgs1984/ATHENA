# Test Quality Rules

## Purpose

Test Quality Rules establish the minimum engineering standards for designing, implementing and maintaining verification assets.

---

## TQ-001 Risk Coverage

Verification effort shall be proportional to the business and technical risk.

---

## TQ-002 Traceability

Every verification activity should trace back to one or more requirements, user stories, defects or engineering changes.

---

## TQ-003 Deterministic Execution

Tests shall produce repeatable results regardless of execution order or environment.

---

## TQ-004 Independence

Verification activities shall not depend on the outcome of other tests.

---

## TQ-005 Maintainability

Verification assets should be easy to understand, modify and extend.

---

## TQ-006 Automation Value

Automate verification only when long-term value exceeds maintenance cost.

---

## TQ-007 Production Fidelity

Critical verification should execute in environments representative of production.

---

## TQ-008 Failure Diagnostics

Every failed verification should provide sufficient information to determine the root cause.

---

## TQ-009 Continuous Improvement

Verification suites should be reviewed regularly to remove obsolete, redundant or unstable tests.

---

## TQ-010 Engineering Confidence

Verification success is measured by confidence, not by the number of executed tests or code coverage alone.
