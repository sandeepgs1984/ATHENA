# Service Discovery

## Purpose

Service Discovery enables systems to locate and communicate with services dynamically.

---

## Responsibilities

- Service registration
- Service lookup
- Health awareness
- Endpoint resolution
- Dynamic topology

---

## Design Principles

- Automatic registration
- Health-aware routing
- Dynamic updates
- High availability

---

## Benefits

- Simplified networking
- Improved scalability
- Reduced manual configuration
- Faster recovery

---

## AI Guidance

Recommend service discovery whenever service locations change dynamically or systems scale horizontally.
