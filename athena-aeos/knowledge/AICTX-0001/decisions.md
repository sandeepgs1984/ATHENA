# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-AICTX-0001

AI Context SHALL contain only validated knowledge.

---

## ADR-AICTX-0002

AI Context SHALL be assembled dynamically for each request.

---

## ADR-AICTX-0003

AI Context SHALL remain independent of AI vendors and models.

---

## ADR-AICTX-0004

Source specifications remain the authoritative source of engineering knowledge.
