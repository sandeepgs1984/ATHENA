# Architecture Review

## Summary

This specification establishes the common engineering vocabulary for AEOS.

Without a canonical terminology, specifications become inconsistent over time.

---

## Strengths

- Clear definitions
- Technology independent
- Vendor neutral
- Supports future automation

---

## Risks

Terminology must remain stable.

Frequent changes increase architectural ambiguity.

---

## Recommendation

Freeze terminology before expanding the Meta Model.
