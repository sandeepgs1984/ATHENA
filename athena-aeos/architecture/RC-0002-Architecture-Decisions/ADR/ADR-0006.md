# ADR-0006 — Repository-first Architecture

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Architecture should be organized as a navigable knowledge repository rather than a collection of disconnected documents.

---

## Decision

AEOS SHALL prioritize repository organization, discoverability, traceability, and consistency.

---

## Consequences

- Better onboarding.
- Easier navigation.
- Improved AI-assisted reasoning.
- Long-term maintainability.
