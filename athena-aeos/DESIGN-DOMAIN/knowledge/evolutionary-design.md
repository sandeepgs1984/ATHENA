# Evolutionary Design

## Purpose

Software design should evolve continuously in response to changing requirements.

Design should support adaptation rather than attempt to predict every future need.

---

## Principles

- Delay irreversible decisions
- Optimize for today's requirements
- Enable incremental extension
- Reduce unnecessary abstraction
- Continuously simplify

---

## Evolution Triggers

- Repeated duplication
- New business capabilities
- Performance bottlenecks
- Operational feedback
- Frequent change requests

---

## AI Guidance

Recommend evolutionary improvements based on observed patterns rather than speculative future scenarios.
