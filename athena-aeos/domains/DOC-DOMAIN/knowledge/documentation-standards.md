# Documentation Standards

Every engineering document SHALL satisfy the following standards.

---

## 1. Clear Purpose

Every document shall begin with a concise description explaining why the document exists.

---

## 2. Ownership

Every document shall identify its owner or owning team.

---

## 3. Version

Every document shall maintain an explicit version.

---

## 4. Scope

The document shall define what is included and excluded.

---

## 5. Consistency

Headings, terminology, formatting, and naming shall remain consistent throughout the document.

---

## 6. Traceability

Where applicable, documentation shall reference:

- Requirements
- ADRs
- APIs
- Components
- Related specifications

---

## 7. Examples

Whenever practical, documents should include working examples.

---

## 8. Review

Engineering documentation shall undergo peer review before publication.

---

## 9. Maintainability

Documentation should be updated whenever implementation changes.

---

## 10. Reusability

Documentation should avoid duplication and instead reference authoritative sources.
