# Design Quality Rules

## Purpose

Design Quality Rules define the engineering standards that every software design should satisfy before implementation.

---

## DQ-001 Business Alignment

Every design decision shall support a business capability.

---

## DQ-002 Simplicity

Prefer the simplest maintainable solution.

---

## DQ-003 Explicit Responsibilities

Every component shall have clearly defined ownership.

---

## DQ-004 High Cohesion

Related behavior should remain together.

---

## DQ-005 Low Coupling

Dependencies should be minimized and intentional.

---

## DQ-006 Stable Interfaces

Public contracts should change less frequently than implementations.

---

## DQ-007 Testability

Design should support independent verification.

---

## DQ-008 Evolvability

Design should enable safe future enhancement.

---

## DQ-009 Readability

Optimize for engineer comprehension.

---

## DQ-010 Evidence-Based Decisions

Major abstractions should have measurable justification.
