# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-MANIFEST-0001

Every published knowledge asset SHALL be registered exactly once.

---

## ADR-MANIFEST-0002

The manifest SHALL reference assets rather than duplicate their content.

---

## ADR-MANIFEST-0003

The manifest SHOULD be generated automatically whenever practical.

---

## ADR-MANIFEST-0004

Specifications remain the authoritative source; the manifest is an inventory.
