# Observability Testing

## Purpose

Observability testing verifies that production systems expose sufficient telemetry to understand behavior, diagnose failures and measure service quality.

A system that cannot be observed cannot be effectively operated.

---

# Verification Areas

- Metrics
- Logs
- Distributed traces
- Health checks
- Alerts
- Dashboards

---

# Questions to Answer

Can engineers determine:

- What failed?
- Why it failed?
- Where it failed?
- Who was impacted?
- How severe is the issue?

---

# Production Readiness

Verify:

- Alert quality
- Monitoring coverage
- Diagnostic information
- Recovery visibility

---

# Engineering Principle

Observability should be designed into the system rather than added after deployment.

---

# AI Guidance

Recommend observability validation whenever introducing new services, infrastructure, integrations or critical business workflows.
