# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-GRAPH-0001

The Knowledge Graph SHALL be generated from repository knowledge.

---

## ADR-GRAPH-0002

Nodes SHALL represent registered knowledge assets.

---

## ADR-GRAPH-0003

Edges SHALL use the canonical relationship vocabulary defined in ONTO-0001.

---

## ADR-GRAPH-0004

The graph SHALL remain a derived representation and never become the source of truth.
