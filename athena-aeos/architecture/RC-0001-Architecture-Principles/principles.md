# Architecture Principles

## AP-001 — Single Responsibility

Every architectural component SHALL have one primary responsibility.

---

## AP-002 — Separation of Concerns

Each layer SHALL address a distinct architectural concern.

---

## AP-003 — Technology Independence

Architecture SHALL remain independent of implementation technologies, vendors, programming languages, AI models, and deployment platforms.

---

## AP-004 — Explicit Ownership

Every architectural artifact SHALL define an owner.

---

## AP-005 — Traceability

Every significant architectural decision SHALL be traceable.

---

## AP-006 — Consistency

Naming, identifiers, metadata, and documentation SHALL remain consistent across the repository.

---

## AP-007 — Extensibility

Architecture SHALL evolve through extension rather than modification whenever practical.

---

## AP-008 — Composability

Architectural components SHOULD be composable into larger capabilities.

---

## AP-009 — Observability

Runtime behavior SHALL be observable through state, events, and traceability.

---

## AP-010 — Stability

Published architectural contracts SHOULD remain stable across versions whenever possible.

---

## AP-011 — Source of Truth

Specifications remain the authoritative source of architectural knowledge.

Derived artifacts SHALL NOT replace source specifications.

---

## AP-012 — Validation

Published architectural artifacts SHALL satisfy repository validation requirements before publication.

---

## Summary

These principles define the architectural philosophy of AEOS and guide future evolution while preserving consistency and maintainability.
