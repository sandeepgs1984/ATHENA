# AI Design Evaluation Contract

## Inputs

Required:

- Requirements
- Existing architecture
- Design proposal
- Constraints

---

## Evaluation Criteria

Assess:

- Simplicity
- Cohesion
- Coupling
- Responsibility Assignment
- Interface Quality
- Dependency Quality
- Maintainability
- Testability
- Extensibility

---

## Evidence Required

Support recommendations using:

- Design principles
- Design metrics
- Trade-off analysis
- Explicit risks

---

## Decision Outcomes

- APPROVE
- APPROVE WITH SUGGESTIONS
- REVISE
- REJECT
- INVESTIGATE FURTHER

---

## Confidence Levels

- High
- Medium
- Low

Every confidence level should include supporting rationale.

---

## Output Format

1. Summary
2. Findings
3. Risks
4. Trade-offs
5. Recommendations
6. Next Actions
