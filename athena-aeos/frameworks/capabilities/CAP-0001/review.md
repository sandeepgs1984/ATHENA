# Architecture Review

## Summary

The Capability Framework introduces reusable engineering functions that are independent of execution and organizational structure.

It provides the foundation for workflows, automation, and AI Agents.

---

## Strengths

- Reusable
- Automation-friendly
- Implementation independent
- Extensible

---

## Risks

Capabilities may become too granular.

Projects should define capability granularity guidelines.

---

## Recommendation

Capabilities should describe business or engineering outcomes rather than implementation details.
