# Architecture Review

## Summary

VALIDATE-0001 introduces a repository-wide validation model.

It improves confidence in AEOS knowledge while remaining independent of implementation.

---

## Strengths

- Deterministic
- Easy to automate
- CI/CD friendly
- Technology independent

---

## Risks

Validation rules should focus on objective checks rather than subjective content quality.

---

## Recommendation

Automate validation wherever possible and integrate it into the repository publication workflow.
