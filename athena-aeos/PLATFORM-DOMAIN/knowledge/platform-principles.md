# Platform Engineering Principles

## Purpose

Platform Principles define the engineering philosophy for building reusable internal platforms.

---

## PP-001 Product Mindset

Treat the platform as a product.

Platform teams should understand user needs, collect feedback and continuously improve the platform experience.

---

## PP-002 Self-Service First

Engineers should be able to provision and use platform capabilities independently whenever possible.

---

## PP-003 Golden Paths

Provide opinionated, well-supported default workflows that encourage best practices while allowing justified exceptions.

---

## PP-004 Reduce Cognitive Load

Hide unnecessary operational complexity behind intuitive abstractions and tooling.

---

## PP-005 Reuse Over Duplication

Centralize common capabilities when they provide measurable value without creating bottlenecks.

---

## PP-006 Secure by Default

Platform capabilities should enforce secure defaults while minimizing additional effort for consuming teams.

---

## PP-007 Observable by Design

Every platform capability should expose health, metrics, logs and traces appropriate to its operational role.

---

## PP-008 Evolutionary Platform

Platform capabilities should evolve incrementally with strong backward compatibility and clear migration paths.

---

## PP-009 Developer Experience Matters

Measure platform success by the productivity and satisfaction of its consumers.

---

## PP-010 Evidence-Based Platform Decisions

Platform investments should be justified using engineering metrics, adoption data and operational feedback.
