# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-GOVDM-0001

Governance SHALL be modeled through specialized governance objects.

---

## ADR-GOVDM-0002

A generic Governance Entity SHALL NOT be introduced.

---

## ADR-GOVDM-0003

Each governance object SHALL define its own lifecycle.

---

## ADR-GOVDM-0004

Governance objects SHALL integrate with TRACE-0001.
