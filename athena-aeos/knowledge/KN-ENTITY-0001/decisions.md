# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-KN-0001

Every Knowledge Object SHALL represent a single knowledge asset.

---

## ADR-KN-0002

Knowledge Objects SHALL reuse the common metadata, ontology, and schema models.

---

## ADR-KN-0003

Knowledge Objects SHALL remain independent of storage format.

---

## ADR-KN-0004

Specifications remain the source of truth; Knowledge Objects are structured representations derived from them.
