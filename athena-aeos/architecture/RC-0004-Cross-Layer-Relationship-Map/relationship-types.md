# Relationship Types

## Defines

Establishes the foundational concept for another artifact.

---

## Enables

Provides capabilities used by another layer.

---

## Governed By

Indicates architectural oversight or policy.

---

## Validates

Represents verification or compliance activities.

---

## Consumed By

Represents knowledge or artifacts used during execution.

---

## Supports

Provides services or capabilities to another layer.

---

## References

Represents a non-dependency conceptual association.
