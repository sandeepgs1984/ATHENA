# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-SPEC-0001

Specifications SHALL be authoritative engineering knowledge.

---

## ADR-SPEC-0002

Specification lifecycle SHALL remain independent of implementation lifecycle.

---

## ADR-SPEC-0003

Specification relationships SHALL use TRACE-0001.

---

## ADR-SPEC-0004

Published specifications SHOULD be immutable, with revisions represented through new versions.
