# Platform Maturity Model

## Purpose

The Platform Maturity Model helps organizations understand how their internal engineering platform evolves over time.

---

## Level 1 — Foundational

- Shared tooling
- Basic automation
- Manual operations

---

## Level 2 — Standardized

- Common templates
- Standard workflows
- Shared services

---

## Level 3 — Self-Service

- Internal Developer Platform
- Automated provisioning
- Golden Paths

---

## Level 4 — Intelligent

- AI-assisted reviews
- Automated recommendations
- Policy validation
- Engineering insights

---

## Level 5 — Autonomous

- AI-driven optimization
- Self-healing workflows
- Predictive operations
- Continuous platform evolution

---

## AI Guidance

Assess maturity objectively and recommend incremental improvements rather than attempting to jump multiple maturity levels at once.
