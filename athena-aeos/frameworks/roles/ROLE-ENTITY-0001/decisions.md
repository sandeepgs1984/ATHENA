# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-0064

Roles SHALL represent responsibilities rather than people.

---

## ADR-0065

Roles MAY be assigned to humans, teams, or AI agents.

---

## ADR-0066

Roles SHALL be reusable.

---

## ADR-0067

Roles SHALL participate in engineering relationships.

---

## ADR-0068

Projects MAY extend canonical roles.
