# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-WF-0001

Workflow SHALL orchestrate rather than execute business logic.

---

## ADR-WF-0002

Workflow SHALL consume Capabilities instead of implementing them.

---

## ADR-WF-0003

Workflow SHALL coordinate Roles but SHALL NOT define Role behavior.

---

## ADR-WF-0004

Workflow execution SHALL remain independent of runtime implementations.
