# Documentation Quality Rules

Every documentation artifact SHALL satisfy the following quality rules.

---

## QR-001 Completeness

Every document shall include all mandatory sections defined by its template.

---

## QR-002 Accuracy

Information shall accurately represent the implemented system.

---

## QR-003 Consistency

Terminology, formatting, and naming shall remain consistent throughout the document.

---

## QR-004 Traceability

Where applicable, documentation shall reference related:

- Requirements
- ADRs
- APIs
- Components
- Source Code

---

## QR-005 Versioning

Every document shall declare its version.

---

## QR-006 Ownership

Every document shall identify an owner.

---

## QR-007 Review

Every published document shall complete an engineering review.

---

## QR-008 Examples

Technical documents should include examples wherever practical.

---

## QR-009 Maintainability

Documentation shall be updated whenever related engineering artifacts change.

---

## QR-010 Accessibility

Documentation should use clear language, logical structure, and descriptive headings.
