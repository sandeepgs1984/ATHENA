# Defect Management

## Purpose

Defect Management ensures issues are identified, prioritized, resolved and prevented from recurring.

The goal is continuous quality improvement rather than issue accumulation.

---

---

# Severity

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

---

# Priority

Business urgency may differ from technical severity.

Prioritization should consider:

- Customer impact
- Business value
- Risk
- Release schedule

---

# Root Cause Analysis

Every significant defect should identify:

- Why it occurred
- Why it escaped
- How recurrence will be prevented

---

# Prevention

Improve:

- Requirements
- Reviews
- Automation
- Test coverage
- Monitoring

---

# AI Guidance

Recommend preventive engineering improvements instead of repeatedly addressing similar defects.
