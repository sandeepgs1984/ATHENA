# Design Review Rules

## Review Checklist

Review:

- Responsibilities
- Module boundaries
- Interfaces
- Coupling
- Cohesion
- Dependencies
- Complexity
- Extensibility
- Testability
- Readability
- Trade-offs
- Risks

---

## Common Questions

- Can this be simpler?
- Is every abstraction justified?
- Is ownership clear?
- Can responsibilities move closer together?
- Will future changes remain localized?

---

## Approval Criteria

Approve when:

- Responsibilities are explicit.
- Cohesion is high.
- Coupling is acceptable.
- Trade-offs are documented.
- Risks are understood.
