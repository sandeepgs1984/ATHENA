# Architecture Review

## Summary

The Engineering Laws establish the immutable rules governing AEOS.

These laws intentionally avoid implementation details and instead define engineering constraints.

---

## Strengths

- Technology independent
- Vendor neutral
- Future proof
- Simple and enforceable
- Clear governance

---

## Risks

Too many constitutional laws may reduce flexibility.

Future laws should remain rare and broadly applicable.

---

## Recommendation

Freeze after approval.

Future additions should only occur through constitutional amendments.

---

Approved
