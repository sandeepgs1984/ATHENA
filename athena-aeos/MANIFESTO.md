# Athena & AEOS Manifesto

## Why this project exists

Software engineering is becoming increasingly AI-assisted.

Current AI tools solve isolated problems.

Athena and AEOS aim to solve engineering as a complete system.

---

Athena is not another chatbot.

Athena is a personal engineering operating environment.

AEOS is not documentation.

AEOS is engineering intelligence.

---

Every capability should satisfy three questions.

Does it improve engineering?

Can it be reused?

Can AI execute it?

If the answer is no, it does not belong inside AEOS.

---

Athena should become the place where engineering happens.

Planning.

Architecture.

Coding.

Reviews.

Testing.

Knowledge.

Automation.

Trading.

Learning.

Everything.

---

Knowledge without execution has limited value.

Execution without knowledge produces inconsistent results.

AEOS exists to combine both.

---

Athena validates AEOS.

AEOS powers Athena.

Both evolve together.

Neither is complete without the other.

---

Build less.

Ship earlier.

Learn continuously.

Improve forever.
