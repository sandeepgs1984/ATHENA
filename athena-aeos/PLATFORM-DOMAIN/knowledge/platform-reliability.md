# Platform Reliability

## Purpose

Platform Reliability ensures engineering capabilities remain dependable, predictable and available.

---

## Reliability Objectives

- Availability
- Durability
- Recoverability
- Fault tolerance
- Capacity planning

---

## Engineering Practices

- Health monitoring
- Automated recovery
- Redundancy
- Disaster recovery
- Incident learning

---

## Metrics

- Availability
- MTTR
- MTBF
- Error budget
- Platform uptime

---

## AI Guidance

Treat reliability as a platform capability rather than an operational afterthought.
