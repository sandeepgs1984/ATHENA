# PLATFORM-DOMAIN Specification

## Domain Purpose

Provide a reusable engineering knowledge base for designing and evolving internal engineering platforms.

---

## Engineering Philosophy

A platform exists to enable product teams—not to centralize control.

The best platform is one that is easy to adopt, difficult to misuse and continuously improves developer productivity.

Platform complexity should be hidden behind simple, well-defined interfaces.

---

## Primary Objectives

- Improve developer experience
- Standardize engineering capabilities
- Reduce duplicated effort
- Improve operational consistency
- Accelerate software delivery
- Simplify platform adoption

---

## Domain Capabilities

- Platform Evaluation
- Capability Planning
- Platform Reviews
- Platform Recommendation
- Platform Governance
- Developer Experience Assessment
- Shared Service Design
- Platform Evolution

---

## Success Metrics

- Faster onboarding
- Reduced engineering toil
- Higher platform adoption
- Improved deployment consistency
- Increased engineering velocity
