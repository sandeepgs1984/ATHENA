# Architecture Review

## Summary

TRACE-0001 introduces a repository-wide relationship model that connects specifications through standardized, directional relationships.

---

## Strengths

- Explicit dependency model.
- Supports scalable governance.
- Enables automated tooling.
- Improves AI reasoning.

---

## Risks

Relationship definitions must remain accurate as the repository evolves.

Future automation should validate relationship consistency.

---

## Recommendation

Every new specification should adopt TRACE-0001 from its initial creation rather than adding traceability retrospectively.
