# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-SCHEMA-0001

Every machine-readable knowledge asset SHALL follow the canonical schema structure.

---

## ADR-SCHEMA-0002

Domain-specific extensions SHALL be contained within the `content` section.

---

## ADR-SCHEMA-0003

The base schema SHALL remain stable and technology-independent.

---

## ADR-SCHEMA-0004

Validation SHALL verify structure before domain-specific rules are applied.
