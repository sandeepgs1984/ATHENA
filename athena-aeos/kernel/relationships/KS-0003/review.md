# Architecture Review

## Summary

The Relationship Service transforms AEOS from a collection of independent entities into an interconnected engineering knowledge graph.

Relationships become governed engineering assets rather than lightweight references.

---

## Strengths

- Graph-native
- Extensible
- Auditable
- Lifecycle-aware
- Runtime independent

---

## Risks

Relationship proliferation may increase graph complexity.

Projects should prefer standardized relationship types.

---

## Recommendation

Keep the canonical relationship taxonomy small.

Domain-specific relationships should be introduced through extension specifications.

---
