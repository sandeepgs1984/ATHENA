# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-KERNEL-0001

The Kernel SHALL remain domain independent.

---

## ADR-KERNEL-0002

Frameworks SHALL consume Kernel Services instead of implementing equivalent functionality.

---

## ADR-KERNEL-0003

Kernel Services SHALL remain loosely coupled.

---

## ADR-KERNEL-0004

New Kernel Services require architectural approval before introduction.

---

## ADR-KERNEL-0005

The Kernel SHALL remain the only shared infrastructure layer within AEOS.
