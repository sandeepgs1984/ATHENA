# Developer Experience (DevEx)

## Purpose

Developer Experience measures how effectively the platform enables engineers to build, test, deploy and operate software.

Platform success is measured by developer productivity—not infrastructure complexity.

---

## DevEx Objectives

- Faster onboarding
- Faster delivery
- Reduced cognitive load
- Better discoverability
- High self-service adoption

---

## Common Friction

- Manual processes
- Poor documentation
- Complex onboarding
- Hidden dependencies
- Slow feedback loops

---

## Success Metrics

- Time to first deployment
- Build success rate
- Deployment frequency
- Platform adoption
- Developer satisfaction

---

## AI Guidance

Recommend improvements that remove repetitive engineering work and reduce cognitive overhead before proposing new platform capabilities.
