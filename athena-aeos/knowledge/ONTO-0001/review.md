# Architecture Review

## Summary

ONTO-0001 establishes a common semantic vocabulary for AEOS.

This specification improves consistency without introducing new engineering concepts.

---

## Strengths

- Clear scope
- Technology independent
- AI-friendly
- Consistent terminology

---

## Risks

The ontology should remain stable. New concepts should only be introduced when broadly applicable.

---

## Recommendation

Treat ONTO-0001 as a constitutional specification. Changes should be infrequent and carefully reviewed.
