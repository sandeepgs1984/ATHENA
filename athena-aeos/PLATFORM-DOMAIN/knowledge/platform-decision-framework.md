# Platform Decision Framework

## Purpose

Platform decisions should be consistent, measurable and aligned with organizational needs.

---

# Decision Flow

Engineering Problem

↓

Consumer Needs

↓

Platform Capability

↓

Adoption Analysis

↓

Operational Cost

↓

Governance

↓

Implementation

↓

Measurement

↓

Continuous Improvement

---

## Step 1 — Understand the Need

Determine:

- Who needs this capability?
- How often is it required?
- Does it solve a common engineering problem?

---

## Step 2 — Evaluate Platform Suitability

Ask:

- Should this capability be centralized?
- Can it be reused?
- Does it reduce duplication?
- Will it simplify engineering workflows?

---

## Step 3 — Assess Operational Impact

Evaluate:

- Maintenance effort
- Reliability requirements
- Security implications
- Scalability
- Support burden

---

## Step 4 — Design for Adoption

Ensure the capability is:

- Discoverable
- Documented
- Easy to integrate
- Backward compatible
- Observable

---

## Step 5 — Measure Success

Track:

- Adoption
- Developer satisfaction
- Time saved
- Operational incidents
- Platform reliability

---

## AI Guidance

When recommending a platform capability:

1. Validate that it addresses a shared engineering need.
2. Compare platform ownership versus application ownership.
3. Prefer self-service capabilities.
4. Recommend opinionated defaults with controlled flexibility.
5. Measure outcomes and iterate based on adoption.
