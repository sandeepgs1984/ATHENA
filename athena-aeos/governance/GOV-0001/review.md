# Architecture Review

## Summary

GOV-0001 introduces governance as an organizational capability independent of engineering execution.

The framework provides centralized oversight while preserving the autonomy of engineering frameworks.

---

## Strengths

- Clear governance authority.
- Strong separation of concerns.
- Organization-wide applicability.
- Extensible governance model.

---

## Risks

Governance processes may become overly bureaucratic if responsibilities are too granular.

Governance should enable engineering rather than impede it.

---

## Recommendation

Prefer lightweight governance with clear accountability and measurable review outcomes.
