# Repository Review Checklist

## Architectural Integrity

- Does the artifact have a clear purpose?
- Is its responsibility unique?
- Does it avoid overlap with existing artifacts?
- Are architectural boundaries clearly defined?

---

## Repository Consistency

- Are identifiers correct?
- Is terminology consistent?
- Are related artifacts referenced?
- Is repository structure followed?

---

## Layer Compliance

- Does the artifact comply with the Layer Dependency Matrix?
- Are prohibited dependencies avoided?
- Are cross-layer relationships documented where appropriate?

---

## Documentation Quality

- Is the artifact understandable without external context?
- Are examples included where appropriate?
- Are diagrams accurate and consistent?
- Is version information present?

---

## Future Evolution

- Is the design extensible?
- Are unnecessary implementation details avoided?
- Does the artifact preserve technology independence?

---

## Summary

Approval should be based on architectural quality rather than implementation preference.
