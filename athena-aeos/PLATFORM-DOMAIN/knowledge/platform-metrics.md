# Platform Metrics

## Purpose

Platform Metrics evaluate whether the engineering platform is delivering measurable value.

---

## Engineering Metrics

- Adoption rate
- Active consumers
- Provisioning time
- Time to first deployment
- Platform availability
- Self-service success rate
- Incident frequency
- Mean resolution time

---

## Business Metrics

- Engineering velocity
- Operational cost
- Platform ROI
- Team satisfaction

---

## AI Guidance

Recommend metrics that reflect platform outcomes rather than infrastructure activity.
