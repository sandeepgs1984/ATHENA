# Validation Principles

## VP-001 — Completeness

Every published artifact SHALL be complete.

---

## VP-002 — Consistency

Repository terminology, identifiers, and metadata SHALL remain consistent.

---

## VP-003 — Traceability

Every architectural artifact SHALL be traceable to its related concepts.

---

## VP-004 — Reviewability

Every artifact SHALL be reviewable without requiring external knowledge.

---

## VP-005 — Versioning

Every published artifact SHALL include version information where applicable.

---

## VP-006 — Stability

Published architectural contracts SHOULD remain stable across releases.

---

## VP-007 — Repository Alignment

Every artifact SHALL align with:

- Architecture Principles
- Global ADRs
- Layer Dependency Matrix
- Cross-Layer Relationship Map

---

## Summary

Validation principles ensure repository-wide architectural quality.
