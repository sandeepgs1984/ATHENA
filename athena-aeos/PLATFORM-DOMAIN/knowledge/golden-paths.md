# Golden Paths

## Purpose

Golden Paths provide opinionated, well-supported engineering workflows that represent the preferred way to build and operate software.

---

## Benefits

- Faster onboarding
- Consistent quality
- Reduced support burden
- Standardized engineering

---

## Characteristics

- Documented
- Automated
- Secure
- Observable
- Supported

---

## Exceptions

Alternative paths may exist when justified by technical or business requirements.

Every exception should document its rationale and operational impact.

---

## AI Guidance

Recommend Golden Paths as defaults, while allowing justified deviations with documented trade-offs.
