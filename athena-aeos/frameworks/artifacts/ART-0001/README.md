# ART-0001 — Artifact Framework

## Overview

The Artifact Framework defines how engineering outputs are represented, classified, governed, versioned, and traced throughout AEOS.

Artifacts are the persistent outcomes of engineering activities and provide the primary mechanism for knowledge exchange between workflows, capabilities, policies, and specifications.

---

## Responsibilities

The Artifact Framework is responsible for:

- Artifact classification
- Artifact lifecycle management
- Artifact provenance
- Artifact traceability
- Artifact discovery
- Artifact governance
