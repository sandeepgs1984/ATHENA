# Domain Modeling

## Purpose

Domain Modeling captures the core business concepts, relationships and behaviors of a problem space.

The model should reflect the business language rather than technical implementation.

---

## Core Concepts

- Entities
- Value Objects
- Aggregates
- Services
- Events
- Policies

---

## Principles

- Shared terminology
- Clear ownership
- Behavioral focus
- Consistency boundaries

---

## Benefits

- Better communication
- Reduced translation between business and engineering
- Improved maintainability

---

## AI Guidance

Recommend domain models that mirror business concepts. Avoid exposing infrastructure concerns within the domain model.
