# Microservices

## Purpose

Microservices divide a system into independently deployable services aligned to business capabilities.

Each service owns its data, lifecycle and deployment.

---

## Advantages

- Independent deployment
- Team autonomy
- Technology flexibility
- Fine-grained scalability
- Fault isolation

---

## Disadvantages

- Operational complexity
- Distributed transactions
- Network latency
- Observability challenges
- Increased infrastructure cost

---

## Best Use Cases

- Large organizations
- Independent product teams
- Rapidly evolving platforms
- Very large-scale systems

---

## Avoid When

- Small engineering teams
- Limited operational maturity
- Premature optimization
- Simple business domains

---

## AI Guidance

Do not recommend Microservices solely for scalability. Recommend them only when organizational independence and deployment autonomy justify the additional complexity.
