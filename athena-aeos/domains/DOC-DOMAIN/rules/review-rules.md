# Documentation Review Rules

These rules define the minimum review criteria for engineering documentation.

---

## RR-001 Purpose

Verify that the document clearly states its purpose.

---

## RR-002 Scope

Verify that the scope is well defined.

---

## RR-003 Technical Correctness

Verify that all technical information is accurate.

---

## RR-004 Terminology

Verify that approved engineering terminology is used consistently.

---

## RR-005 References

Verify that referenced documents, ADRs, APIs, and diagrams are valid.

---

## RR-006 Examples

Verify that examples are correct and executable where applicable.

---

## RR-007 Formatting

Verify that the document follows approved formatting standards.

---

## RR-008 Diagrams

Verify that diagrams accurately represent the documented architecture or workflow.

---

## RR-009 Metadata

Verify that metadata is complete.

Required metadata includes:

- Title
- Version
- Status
- Owner
- Last Updated

---

## RR-010 Approval

Verify that the document has been reviewed and approved before publication.
