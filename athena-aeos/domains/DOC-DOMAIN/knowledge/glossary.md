# Documentation Glossary

This glossary defines common terminology used throughout the Documentation Domain.

---

## ADR

Architecture Decision Record.

Documents an important engineering decision together with its rationale and consequences.

---

## Artifact

A version-controlled engineering deliverable.

Examples:

- Specification
- Design Document
- API Contract
- Runbook
- Diagram

---

## Capability

A reusable engineering function that provides standardized behavior.

Examples:

- Review
- Validation
- Generation
- Planning

---

## Domain

A collection of engineering knowledge related to a specific engineering discipline.

Examples:

- Documentation
- Code
- API
- Testing

---

## Engineering Package

The smallest independently versioned engineering unit conforming to EPS-0001.

---

## Knowledge Asset

Reusable engineering knowledge consumed by capabilities.

Examples:

- Standards
- Rules
- Templates
- ADRs
- Best Practices

---

## Metadata

Structured information describing an engineering artifact.

---

## Review

Evaluation of an engineering artifact for quality, completeness, and consistency.

---

## Validation

Verification that an artifact complies with applicable engineering rules.

---

## Traceability

The ability to connect an engineering artifact to its originating requirements, design decisions, and implementation.
