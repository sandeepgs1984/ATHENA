# Multi-Tenancy

## Purpose

Multi-tenancy enables a shared engineering platform to safely serve multiple independent teams, business units or products while maintaining isolation, governance and operational efficiency.

---

## Isolation Levels

- Logical isolation
- Namespace isolation
- Resource isolation
- Data isolation
- Security isolation

---

## Design Principles

- Strong tenant boundaries
- Independent lifecycle management
- Resource fairness
- Configurable policies
- Auditability

---

## Trade-offs

Advantages

- Lower operational cost
- Better resource utilization
- Shared capabilities

Challenges

- Noisy neighbors
- Security boundaries
- Operational complexity

---

## AI Guidance

Recommend multi-tenancy only when multiple consumers benefit from shared platform capabilities without compromising isolation requirements.
