# Architecture Review

## Summary

The Reference Architecture establishes the structural blueprint of AEOS.

It separates constitutional specifications from implementation concerns and provides clear dependency rules for future evolution.

---

## Strengths

- Clear layering
- Stable responsibilities
- Strong separation of concerns
- Scalable architecture
- Vendor-neutral design

---

## Risks

Layer responsibilities must remain stable to avoid architectural drift.

---

## Recommendation

Freeze after approval.

Future enhancements should extend layers rather than create new ones.
