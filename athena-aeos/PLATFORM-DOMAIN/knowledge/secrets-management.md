# Secrets Management

## Purpose

Secrets Management protects sensitive credentials while enabling secure application access.

---

## Secret Types

- API Keys
- Tokens
- Passwords
- Certificates
- Encryption Keys

---

## Principles

- Least privilege
- Short-lived credentials
- Rotation
- Auditing
- Encryption
- Centralized management

---

## Avoid

- Hardcoded secrets
- Shared credentials
- Long-lived tokens
- Plain text storage

---

## AI Guidance

Recommend centralized secrets management with automated rotation and access auditing.
