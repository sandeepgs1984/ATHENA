# Architectural Decisions

## ADR-PIPELINE-0001

Execution Pipelines SHALL define execution order without performing execution.

---

## ADR-PIPELINE-0002

Pipeline stages SHALL be independently executable.

---

## ADR-PIPELINE-0003

Execution context SHALL be preserved throughout the pipeline lifecycle.

---

## ADR-PIPELINE-0004

Pipeline execution SHALL be delegated to the Execution Engine under orchestration control.
