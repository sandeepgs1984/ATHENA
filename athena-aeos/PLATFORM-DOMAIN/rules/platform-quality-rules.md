# Platform Quality Rules

## Purpose

Platform Quality Rules define the engineering standards that every shared platform capability should satisfy before adoption.

---

## PQ-001 Consumer Value

Every platform capability shall solve a shared engineering problem.

---

## PQ-002 Self-Service

Capabilities should be consumable with minimal platform team involvement.

---

## PQ-003 Opinionated Defaults

Provide well-supported default workflows while allowing justified exceptions.

---

## PQ-004 Backward Compatibility

Changes should minimize disruption for consuming teams.

---

## PQ-005 Secure by Default

Security controls should be enabled without additional consumer effort.

---

## PQ-006 Observable by Design

Capabilities shall expose logs, metrics, traces and health information.

---

## PQ-007 Reliability

Platform services should meet defined availability objectives.

---

## PQ-008 Discoverability

Capabilities should be documented and easily discoverable.

---

## PQ-009 Measurable Outcomes

Success should be measured using adoption and productivity metrics.

---

## PQ-010 Continuous Improvement

Platform capabilities should evolve using operational feedback and engineering data.
