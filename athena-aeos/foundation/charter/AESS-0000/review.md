# Architecture Review

## Summary

The AEOS Charter establishes the constitutional foundation of the platform.

Rather than describing implementation details, it defines the immutable direction that all future engineering work must follow.

---

## Strengths

- Vendor-neutral
- Technology-independent
- Long-term vision
- Clear governance hierarchy
- Stable constitutional scope

---

## Risks

- Broad scope requires disciplined future specifications.
- Constitutional changes should remain extremely rare.

---

## Recommendations

Freeze after approval.

Future enhancements should be introduced through lower-level specifications whenever possible.
