# Platform Review Rules

## Review Checklist

Review:

- Consumer value
- Shared ownership
- Platform fit
- Security
- Reliability
- Developer Experience
- Operational cost
- Scalability
- Governance
- Adoption strategy

---

## Review Questions

- Should this capability belong in the platform?
- Can multiple teams benefit?
- Does it reduce duplicated effort?
- Is adoption simple?
- Is operational ownership clear?
- Is there a Golden Path?

---

## Approval Criteria

Approve when:

- Consumer value is demonstrated.
- Operational ownership is defined.
- Adoption strategy exists.
- Security and reliability are acceptable.
- Platform metrics are defined.
