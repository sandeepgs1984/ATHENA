# Design Trade-offs

## Purpose

Every software design optimizes certain qualities while sacrificing others.

Trade-offs should be explicit and intentional.

---

## Common Trade-offs

### Flexibility vs Simplicity

Additional extension points improve flexibility but increase complexity.

---

### Performance vs Maintainability

Optimized implementations may become harder to understand.

---

### Reuse vs Duplication

Premature reuse can create unnecessary abstraction.

Limited duplication may be preferable until stable patterns emerge.

---

### Generalization vs Specificity

General solutions support broader use but increase cognitive load.

---

## AI Guidance

Present multiple viable options when trade-offs are balanced. Explain why a recommendation fits the current context.
