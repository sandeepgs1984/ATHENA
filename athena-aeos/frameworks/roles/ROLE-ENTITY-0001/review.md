# Architecture Review

## Summary

The Role Entity provides a reusable engineering responsibility model independent of organizations and implementation technologies.

---

## Strengths

- Reusable
- Governed
- Automation friendly
- Human and AI compatible

---

## Risks

Projects may over-specialize roles.

Canonical roles should remain stable.

---

## Recommendation

Prefer extension over modification.

---

## Review Status

Approved for Draft

---

## Meta Model Compliance

This specification now conforms to MM-0001.

Common engineering properties have been removed in favor of canonical Entity inheritance.

The resulting specification is smaller, clearer, and easier to maintain.
