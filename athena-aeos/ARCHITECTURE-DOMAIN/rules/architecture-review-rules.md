# Architecture Review Rules

## Purpose

Architecture reviews ensure that engineering decisions are technically sound, aligned with business goals and maintainable over time.

---

# Review Checklist

Review:

- Business objectives
- Constraints
- Quality attributes
- Architecture style
- Data flow
- Integration strategy
- Scalability
- Reliability
- Security
- Observability
- Deployment model
- Cost implications
- Team capability

---

# Common Questions

- Is the architecture unnecessarily complex?
- Can a simpler alternative achieve the same outcome?
- Are quality attributes explicitly prioritized?
- Are operational concerns addressed?
- Is technical debt acceptable?
- Are future evolution paths documented?

---

# Approval Criteria

Architecture should not proceed unless:

- Trade-offs are documented.
- Risks are understood.
- Major quality attributes are satisfied.
- Operational impact is acceptable.
- Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) are completed.
